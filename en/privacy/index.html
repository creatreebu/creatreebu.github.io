Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who is responsible

CREATREEBU, a graphic design studio run by Pablo Martín Maldonado, based in La Costa, Buenos Aires Province, Argentina. For any question about this policy write to creatreebu@gmail.com.

2. What data is used

This site has no user accounts, no database of its own and installs no tracking cookies. The contact form does not send information to our servers: it builds a message that opens in WhatsApp and is only sent when you send it.

When you write to us on WhatsApp or by email we receive your name, your number or email address and whatever you tell us in the message.

3. What we use it for

Only to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and coordinate the work if you decide to go ahead. We do not sell, rent or hand your data to third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log technical data about the visit, such as the IP address.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces and receives your IP address when loading them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Gmail (Google) process the messages you send us through them, under their own policies.
  • Links to Facebook, Google and YouTube take you to external sites governed by their own privacy policies.

The site stores a temporary flag in your browser (sessionStorage) so the intro animation is not repeated; it is cleared when you close the tab and holds no personal data.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your enquiry or the agreed work, and for any period required by law. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction, updating or deletion of your data by writing to creatreebu@gmail.com, in line with Argentine Law No. 25,326 on Personal Data Protection.

The Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the supervisory body of Law No. 25,326, is authorised to handle complaints and claims from anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules in force.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update it, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
