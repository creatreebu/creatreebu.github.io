      FAQ

      Questions

      Frequently asked questions about graphic design

      What people usually ask before hiring. If you have another question, write to us.

      What graphic design services does CREATREEBU offer?

      Visual identity and brand graphic systems; commercial and promotional graphic design; flyers, signage, brochures and advertising pieces; posts, stories and campaigns for social media; packaging, labels and editorial design; and banners, presentations and digital communication pieces.

      What does the Commercial Express Pack include?

      Five original graphic pieces for social media, adaptation to the formats we agree on, application of your existing visual identity and two rounds of revisions.

      How much does a design job cost?

      We do not work with list prices because every project has a different scope. You message us on WhatsApp, tell us what you need and we send you a written quote before we start.

      How long does a job take?

      It depends on the scope and on when we receive the materials and your feedback. The estimated timeline is agreed in writing before we start, once we know the job.

      What materials do I need to send?

      Your logo and, if you have them, colors and typefaces; photos or material of your product, shop or accommodation; and the text or details of what you want to communicate. If anything is missing, we work it out together in the initial consultation.

      How many rounds of revisions are included?

      The Commercial Express Pack includes two rounds of revisions. For other jobs, the number of revisions is agreed in the scope before we start.

      Do you work with businesses in La Costa and in other cities?

      Yes. We work from La Costa, Buenos Aires Province, and serve businesses and brands across Argentina remotely. Everything is coordinated online, via WhatsApp.

      What files do I receive at the end of the job?

      The final files, ready to publish or print, in the formats we agree on in the scope, for example images for social media or print-shop files.

      Are the portfolio projects for real clients?

      Some are, some are not. Projects labeled “Concept project” are fictional brands created to show how we work on identity, packaging, editorial design, social media and promotions. The rest are jobs or proposals for real clients.
